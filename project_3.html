  • Web traffic digital analytics by Google Analytics - GA4 (key words: Web Traffic, Google Analytics, HTTP, Cookies, Consent, Looker Studio)

    • I don’t have sufficient data to quantify the difference, but based on my experience, digital marketing can often be more measurable and cost-efficient than conventional marketing channels such as TV advertising. Imagine that I have a website, which I actually do, or an online store. With the help of web traffic analytics, I can answer three fundamental questions:

    1. Who are my visitors (and how many)? (Acquisition)
    2. What do they do on my website (and how many)? (Behaviour)
    3. Who became my customers eventually (and the ratio)? (Conversion)

    In other words, web analytics allows me to follow the customer journey from acquisition to behaviour and ultimately conversion, while measuring each stage with data.

    In this project, I created a funnel model with GA4 where the user data is collected from a personal website. The process can be transferable to most organization or company websites. 

    		My steps are:
		1. Build up a website (to plant the GA tags to collect user data)
		2. Create a Google Analytic account on https://analytics.google.com
		3. Create a property under this account
		4. Create a data stream under each property
		5. Plant the tags (gtag.js) into the web pages
		6. Configure the consent settings
		7. Collect users' data and analyze in GA4
		8. Output to Looker Studio for better user visualization & interaction


    All is hosted on my GitHub

    Furthermore, for most schools’ admission processes, even though they might have established funnel models for the analytics of application – admission – registration cycle, all of that happened to the users' data we’ve already acquired.
